On a proprietary binary forecasting task (a few hundred target training examples; a related source sample more than an order of magnitude larger), centring the ridge at the source fit improved held-out log-loss by 0.0056 nats (0.3161 -> 0.3105) and ECE from 0.030 to 0.027 over the target-only fit chosen by an extensive cross-validated search (paired block-bootstrap SE of the log-loss delta 0.0032, so about 1.7 SE; the 95 % interval, -0.0120 to +0.0007, includes 0). Cross-validation chose a lambda so large that the fit stayed within 0.003 of the source parameters: at this target size the gain came from the source fit, and target adaptation added nothing measurable. Against an earlier, lightly tuned target-only configuration the difference was within noise (paired SE 0.0005-0.0009).
